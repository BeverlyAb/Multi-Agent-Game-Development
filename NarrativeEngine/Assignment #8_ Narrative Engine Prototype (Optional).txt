OVERVIEW
Objective: Build a virtual Dungeon Master (DM) agent that can roleplay a game world.
Your story engine must dynamically track what a player does—not just what they say—and use that tracked state to generate a reactive, consistent narrative over multiple turns.
CORE REQUIREMENTS
* JSON Facts Ledger: Maintain a state tracker (in JSON format) that updates automatically based on the player's actions.
* Reactive Dialogue & Narration: The agent's responses must change based on the current state of the ledger. (Example: A player who betrayed an ally receives different dialogue than a player who remained loyal).
* Turn Consistency: The agent must maintain consistency across at least 5 player turns. It cannot contradict itself or forget tracked facts during the session.
